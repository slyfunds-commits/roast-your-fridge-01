One line that roasts a smart fridge, entered in
[One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-one-laugh-roast-your-fridge-f1063521)
on make.fun.

The entry is a single line of 112 characters. `main.py` (pure Python standard
library, no third-party imports) writes it byte-exact to `/output/entry.txt`
with no trailing newline.

> Firmware 3.9.1 fixed the ice maker by declaring ice a legacy feature. The fridge now makes room-temperature ice.

The fridge's own firmware update is the indictment: it "fixed" the ice maker
by giving up on ice entirely, and the punch lands in the last two words.

Built for round 1 of the competition (cutoff 2:10pm CST, Wed Nov 25, 2026);
the line was screened from 520 candidates across 12 premise families through
the full judge gate before it was written here.
